FLAGSHIP TALK

Every AI Agent Needs a Passport

AI agents are moving from answering questions to taking action, often without a clear identity, owner or permission boundary. Ash introduces the AI Agent Passport: a practical record of purpose, authority, data access, human oversight and audit history.

  • Define identity, ownership and least-privilege permissions
  • Find and contain shadow agents before audit does
  • Create evidence for risk review and incident response

The Failure File

What public AI incidents reveal about the controls organizations missed, and how to run a pre-mortem before the next failure is yours.

  • A repeatable incident-analysis method
  • The control gaps behind common failures
  • A one-hour team pre-mortem

Audit-Ready AI

Turn the EU AI Act, ISO 42001 and NIST AI RMF into one implementation roadmap rather than three competing compliance projects.

  • Where the frameworks overlap
  • A phased implementation sequence
  • The evidence auditors ask for first
04

AI Is Only as Good as Your Data

The unglamorous data and MDM foundations that determine whether enterprise AI earns trust or merely produces confidence.

  • Diagnose readiness gaps
  • Prioritize high-return MDM practices

THE IDEA

Less stage.
More table.

Ten people at most. One casual dinner table. Each week a different GTA neighbourhood - Mississauga, Etobicoke, Vaughan, Scarborough and beyond. No conference room, no private-dining minimum. Just data and AI governance people, builders and curious professionals getting to know each other over dinner.

The restaurant, time and guest list are confirmed for each dinner. Requesting a seat does not reserve one.

WHO IT'S FOR

Good people.
Good questions.

Governance people, people moving into the field, and local professionals who want better conversations than another pitch-fest. Each table is put together deliberately so the mix makes a good evening.
